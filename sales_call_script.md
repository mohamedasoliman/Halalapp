## CALL TIPS

- **Best time to call:** 2-4pm (after lunch rush, before dinner prep)
- **Lead with $10 tier** — it's the sweet spot. If they hesitate, drop to $5. If they're keen, upsell to $15
- **Restaurants with missing logos are your warmest leads** — *"I noticed your listing doesn't have a photo yet, let me fix that for you"*
- **Speak their language** if possible — Urdu, Arabic, Turkish, Malay
- **The Halal Kiwi Pages angle is your urgency creator** — *"launching in 2 weeks, early bird pricing won't last"*
- **"Did you know you were on there?"** — this one question creates curiosity and gets them to check the app
- **Don't sell on first call if they resist** — get the email, follow up in a week
- **Track calls** in a spreadsheet: name, number, outcome, follow-up date, tier interested in

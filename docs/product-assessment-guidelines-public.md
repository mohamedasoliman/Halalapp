# How Products Are Assessed

**Public draft**

This page explains how product statuses are decided. It is intended to help users make an
informed choice. It is not a fatwa, and users may ask a trusted local scholar when they
follow a different recognised opinion.

## Product Statuses

- **Halal:** The available evidence meets our assessment rules.
- **Not Halal:** The evidence confirms a prohibited ingredient, source or process under
  our rules.
- **Under Review:** We do not yet have enough reliable information to decide.
- **Mashbooh:** We investigated the product, but an important halal concern remains
  unresolved.

Under Review and Mashbooh do not mean that a product is Not Halal.

## How We Check Products

We assess the exact product and barcode. We may use:

- a current halal certificate covering the product and manufacturing site;
- written information from the manufacturer;
- official ingredients and product specifications;
- details about slaughter, processing, cleaning and shared production lines; and
- current product packaging.

We do not give one product the same status as another simply because their names,
packaging or ingredients look similar. A statement about one product applies to a whole
range only when the manufacturer clearly confirms that scope.

## Halal Certificates

- Current certificates from FIANZ, NZIDT and JAKIM are accepted when they can be verified
  and cover the exact product and manufacturing site.
- Overseas certificates are accepted when the certifier is currently recognised by JAKIM
  and the certificate's scope is verified.
- HCS-certified products are reviewed individually, especially meat and poultry, because
  the actual production and slaughter process must meet our rules.
- Other or unverifiable certificates require further review.
- A halal logo by itself is not enough if it cannot be authenticated.
- A certificate does not override a prohibited ingredient or an unacceptable process.

## Meat and Poultry

- Meat and poultry require clear evidence of an acceptable halal slaughter process.
- Meat is Not Halal when halal slaughter cannot be confirmed.
- Mechanically slaughtered poultry is Not Halal without individual confirmation that
  every bird is alive at the time of slaughter.
- Scholars differ on mechanical slaughter. Users who follow another recognised opinion
  may ask a trusted local scholar.
- Gelatine, animal rennet, animal fat, stock and similar ingredients require a confirmed
  halal source.

## Ingredients and Processing

- Pork, lard, blood and ingredients from prohibited animals are Not Halal.
- Carmine, cochineal and E120 are treated as Not Halal.
- A normal seasoning amount of nutmeg in a mixed food is acceptable when every other
  ingredient and process is acceptable.
- Pure nutmeg and concentrated nutmeg extracts, oils or supplements are Not Halal.
- Vegetarian or vegan labelling does not automatically make a product Halal.
- An exact-product statement from its manufacturer that it is halal suitable can support
  Halal status unless reliable product evidence shows a conflicting prohibited ingredient
  or process.
- Shared production equipment is acceptable when the manufacturer confirms cleaning or
  sanitation between runs.

## Alcohol and Ethanol

- Wine, beer, sake, liqueur and other intoxicating drinks used as ingredients are Not
  Halal. Cooking does not change this decision.
- Non-intoxicating traces created naturally in food are acceptable.
- Ethanol used only as a processing aid, solvent or flavour carrier can be acceptable when
  there is no practical halal alternative, it is not sourced from an alcoholic drink, and
  the finished product cannot intoxicate.
- If a real ethanol concern remains unresolved after investigation, the product is
  Mashbooh rather than automatically Not Halal.

## Evidence and Product Notes

The full evidence and decision history are kept privately for auditing. Product notes shown
in the app are kept short and explain only what is useful to the user. They do not include
internal IDs, dates or evidence-storage locations.

Product ingredients, manufacturing methods and certificates can change. We reassess a
product when reliable new information becomes available and correct its status when
necessary.
